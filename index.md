## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Non-Personal Gameplay Data
The Game is primarily designed to function as an offline experience. To provide core gameplay features, the following data is stored locally on your device:
- **Game Progress**: High scores, total distance traveled, and Gacha results.
- **Save Encryption**: This data is encrypted using industry-standard techniques and remains exclusively on your device. We do not have access to your local save files.

### 1.2 Data We Do Not Directly Collect
We do not operate an account system or private analytics server, and we do **not directly** collect names, phone numbers, contact lists, or precise geographic location. Third-party services integrated into the Game may process device identifiers, coarse location, advertising data, product interaction data, performance data, and crash data as described below and in the privacy disclosures shown on the applicable app store.

### 1.3 Device Permissions (Photo Library)
The Game may request permission to access your **Photo Library**.
- **Purpose**: This permission is used exclusively to allow you to select an image from your device to be used within the game (e.g., for custom summoning features or avatars).
- **Data Handling**: All image processing is performed **locally on your device**. We do **NOT** collect, store on our servers, or transmit your photos or any metadata associated with your images to any third party.

## Data Deletion Request
We use **Google Play Games Services** and **Apple Game Center** to provide Leaderboard features. We do not host your personal account data on our private servers. If you wish to request the deletion of your game-related data (such as high scores or associated User IDs), please follow these steps:

*   **Method 1 (Self-Service)**:
    *   **Android**: Open the Google Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data > Select this game to remove data.
    *   **iOS**: Go to Settings > Game Center > Manage Game Center Data (or Sign Out) to manage your cloud data.
*   **Local game data**: Deleting the Game removes locally stored progress from that device unless it has been restored from a device backup.
*   **Support request**: You may email **evernamgreen@proton.me** with the subject **"Data Request - Void Summoner: Dark Run"** for assistance. Because leaderboard and advertising data are processed by Apple, Google, or other platform providers rather than on our own servers, some requests must be completed through the applicable provider's privacy controls.
